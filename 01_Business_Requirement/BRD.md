# Smart Inventory Management System

## 1. Project Overview

### Company
**NovaMart Retail Pvt. Ltd.**

### Business Model
NovaMart is an omnichannel consumer products company operating through:

- E-commerce website/app
- 10 physical retail stores
- 3 regional warehouses
- 1 central e-commerce fulfillment center

### Project
**Smart Inventory Management System**

### Project Purpose
The purpose of this project is to design a data-driven inventory management solution that helps NovaMart balance product availability with inventory cost.

The solution will analyze sales, inventory, supplier, warehouse, store, and order data to identify inventory risks and support decisions such as:

- Reorder inventory
- Transfer inventory between locations
- Rebalance inventory
- Investigate inventory/data/fulfillment issues

### Primary Business Objective
> **Balance product availability and inventory cost by ensuring the right products are available at the right location and time, while minimizing unnecessary inventory investment.**

## 2. Business Problem

NovaMart has identified a potential loss of sales because customers have attempted to purchase products that were unavailable at the time of demand.

The underlying cause of these availability failures is currently unclear. Potential causes may include insufficient physical inventory, inefficient inventory allocation across locations, inaccurate or incomplete inventory data, or technical and fulfillment issues across the e-commerce and retail channels.

The purpose of this project is to identify the root causes of product unavailability and determine the most appropriate business action, such as replenishing inventory, transferring stock between locations, improving inventory allocation, or addressing data and technology issues.

The analysis will also examine excess and slow-moving inventory to ensure that improving product availability does not unnecessarily increase inventory holding costs.
